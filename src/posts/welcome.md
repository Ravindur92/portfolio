Hi, I'm Ravindu. This is where I'll share write-ups and notes from my cybersecurity work, mainly around malware analysis, network security and incident response.

## How I structure write-ups

I follow the same shape as a real security report:

- **Scope**: what I was testing or analysing
- **Recon**: how I mapped it
- **Finding**: what I discovered and how
- **Impact**: what an attacker could do with it
- **Remediation**: how to fix or defend against it

All work here is done on my own lab systems or practice environments.
